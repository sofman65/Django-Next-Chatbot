# DoChat.ai – Branding Playbook & Page Copy

---
## 1 Brand Essence
**Mission**  Empower every team to turn conversation into action—instantly and intelligently.

**Tagline**  *Talk less. Do more.*

**Value Proposition**  DoChat.ai is the AI‑enhanced chat workspace that captures decisions, auto‑generates next steps, and keeps projects moving—so teams can focus on building, not typing.

**Brand Personality**  
• *Practical Visionary* – pairs big‑picture innovation with hands‑on utility  
• *Candid* – clear, concise, jargon‑free  
• *Empathetic* – understands real‑world team pain points  
• *Optimistic* – forward‑looking, solution‑oriented

---
## 2 Voice & Tone Guidelines
| Pillar | What it means | Example |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| **Concise Clarity** | Say the most with the fewest words | “One click to an instant meeting summary.” |
| **Action‑oriented** | Emphasize outcomes, not features | “Turn chat into tasks automatically.” |
| **Empathetic Coach** | Cheer users on, never judge | “We’ve got your back—here’s the gist.” |
| **Confident, not cocky** | Avoid hypey superlatives | ❌ “World‑dominating.” ✅ “AI that frees your focus.” |

*Words we love*: streamline, capture, instant, focus, next step, clarity  
*Words we dodge*: revolutionize, disruptor, synergy, leverage (as a verb)

### 3.3 Logo
- Word‑mark in Primary Blue on light or White on Deep Navy.  
- Maintain ×1 logo height clear space.  
- Minimum width 120 px.

### 3.4 Imagery & Illustration
- Abstract line illustrations echoing chat bubbles & flow lines.  
- Photography: candid team collaboration shots, cool‑toned lighting.  
- Icons: 1.5‑px stroke, rounded corners, 24 px grid.

## 5 Login & Authentication Flow
### 5.1 Login Page
- **Header**: “Welcome back to DoChat”  
- **Tagline**: “Your conversations, super‑powered.”  
- **Form**:  
  - Email (placeholder: “name@company.com”)  
  - Password  
  - **Button**: “Log in →”  
  - Divider with “or”  
  - SSO buttons: “Continue with Google”, “Continue with Microsoft”  
- **Links**: “Forgot password?” · “New here? Sign up →”  
- **Security footnote**: “We use industry‑standard encryption to protect your credentials.”

### 5.2 Signup Highlights
- Benefit header: “Start turning talk into tasks today.”  
- Steps: 1 Create account, 2 Connect workspace, 3 Invite team.  
- Checkbox: “I agree to the Terms & Privacy.”

### 5.3 Error & Success States
| State | Message | Tone |
|-------|---------|------|
| **Invalid creds** | “Hmm… that email & password didn’t match.” | Friendly, instructive |
| **Password reset sent** | “Reset link is on its way—check *name@company.com*.” | Reassuring |
| **Rate limit** | “Too many attempts. Try again in 2 minutes.” | Firm, empathetic |

### 5.4 Loading & Empty States
- *“Warming up the AI…”* (skeleton UI)  
- Empty chat: “It’s quiet in here—say hello to start collaborating!”
